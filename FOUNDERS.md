# AmniShield Founding Supporters & Journey

Welcome to the official record of **AmniShield Founding Supporters**.

## 1. Our Mission and Journey

AmniShield was created with a clear, singular purpose: **to provide individuals with uncompromising, on-device digital discipline and focus tools without sacrificing personal privacy**.

In an era where attention is monetized and digital distractions are intentionally engineered to bypass human willpower, AmniShield serves as an impenetrable shield:
- **Zero Telemetry**: All accessibility inspection, blocking engines, and usage stats run 100% locally on your device.
- **Offline Cryptographic Licensing**: Features and passes are validated using NIST P-256 ECDSA public-key cryptography without tracking user accounts or querying remote licensing servers.
- **Deep System Hardening**: Triple-PIN security architecture, anti-uninstall protection, and tamper detection prevent impulsive self-sabotage.
- **Free and Open-Source Core**: Core blocking capabilities remain accessible to everyone, supported by our early founding community.
